03 / BUILD

Kang Parkir Simulator

A systems-heavy Roblox simulator built around parking operations, progression, monetization, social competition, and responsive player feedback.

RoleGame Developer
PlatformRoblox
FocusSystems · Economy · UX
LanguageLua
GitHub Profile ↗
01 / Product

A loop that has to stay readable and fun

The core experience combines claiming a parking lot, handling customers, collecting fees, chasing escape events, progression, and social competition. Each mechanic has to communicate state quickly so the player understands what to do next.

02 / Systems

Design multiple systems as one product

01
Economy Loop

Parking income, vehicle progression, rewards, and monetization mechanics.

02
Interaction

Whistle, chase, combat, PvP lots, and contextual gameplay states.

03
UX

Responsive UI behavior designed to remain usable across desktop and mobile screens.

03 / Outcome

A practical product-building laboratory

The project strengthened product thinking beyond code: balancing loops, validating UI, handling edge cases, and repeatedly adjusting systems based on how they feel in play rather than how they look on paper. The key art also helps the case study feel like a complete shipped product instead of only a technical prototype.
