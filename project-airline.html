U.S. Airline Operations & Delay Intelligence

An operational intelligence dashboard built to make airline reliability, cancellations, and delay patterns readable at a glance.

RoleData Analyst / BI
ToolsTableau · Data Cleaning · Analysis
Dataset10,000-flight 2024 sample

View Tableau Public ↗GitHub Profile ↗
01 / Problem

From flight records to an operational story

Raw operational data is useful only when decision-makers can quickly see which carriers are reliable, where delays originate, and how cancellations change the picture. The dashboard reframes those records around operational questions rather than raw columns.

02 / Approach

Design the dashboard around decisions

The analysis was structured around headline reliability KPIs, carrier comparisons, cancellation behavior, delay causes, and supporting trends. Visual hierarchy keeps the most actionable signals visible without turning the dashboard into a wall of charts.

03 / Outcome

A clearer operational comparison

The final result provides a compact view of carrier performance while still allowing deeper inspection of why delays occur. It demonstrates the full analytics loop: preparing data, defining metrics, and communicating an operational narrative.
